# Teaching mission format

```md
# Mission: <topic>

## Why
<the concrete real-world outcome>

## Success looks like
- <observable capability>

## Constraints
- <time, budget, prior knowledge, or preference>

## Out of scope
- <explicit boundary>
```

Keep one mission per learning workspace. A mission is a compass, not a lesson
plan; revise it only after the human confirms that the real goal changed.
